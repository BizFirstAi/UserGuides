Group Operations

resource: group — group lifecycle, membership, admins, and broadcast

create

Creates a WhatsApp group.

FieldRequiredDescription
subject✓ YesGroup name/subject.
participantPhoneNumbers✓ YesComma-separated list of E.164 phone numbers to add as initial members.

get

Fetches group details.

FieldRequiredDescription
groupId✓ YesGroup ID.

delete

Deletes/disbands a group.

FieldRequiredDescription
groupId✓ YesGroup ID.

update-name

Renames a group.

FieldRequiredDescription
groupId✓ YesGroup ID.
subject✓ YesNew group name.

get-members

Lists current group members.

FieldRequiredDescription
groupId✓ YesGroup ID.

add-member

Adds a participant to a group.

FieldRequiredDescription
groupId✓ YesGroup ID.
phoneNumber✓ YesE.164 phone number to add.

remove-member

Removes a participant from a group.

FieldRequiredDescription
groupId✓ YesGroup ID.
phoneId✓ YesParticipant's phone/member ID to remove.

promote-admin

Promotes a member to group admin.

FieldRequiredDescription
groupId✓ YesGroup ID.
phoneId✓ YesMember ID to promote.

demote-admin

Demotes a group admin to a regular member.

FieldRequiredDescription
groupId✓ YesGroup ID.
phoneId✓ YesMember ID to demote.

get-invite-link

Returns the group's invite link.

FieldRequiredDescription
groupId✓ YesGroup ID.

send-message

Sends a text message to a group.

FieldRequiredDescription
groupId✓ YesGroup ID.
text✓ YesMessage body.
Groups are outside the standard Cloud API messaging surface: group operations depend on WhatsApp Business Account group-management capabilities that Meta gates separately from standard one-to-one/business messaging — availability can vary by WABA and API tier. Confirm your account has group management enabled before building a workflow around this resource.
