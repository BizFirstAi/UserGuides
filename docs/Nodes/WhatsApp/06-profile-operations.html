Profile Operations

resource: profile — contact, business profile, and account info

get-contact

Looks up a contact's WhatsApp profile.

FieldRequiredDescription
contactPhoneNumber✓ YesPhone number of the contact to look up.

update-contact-name

Updates the display name stored for a contact.

FieldRequiredDescription
contactPhoneNumber✓ YesPhone number of the contact.
displayName✓ YesNew display name.

get-business-profile

Returns the business profile (about, address, description, email, websites, vertical) for the sending phone number.

update-business-profile

Updates one or more business profile fields.

FieldRequiredDescription
aboutNoShort "About" text.
addressNoBusiness address.
descriptionNoBusiness description.
emailNoContact email.
websitesNoComma-separated list of website URLs.
verticalNoIndustry vertical, e.g. RETAIL.

get-account-info

Returns WhatsApp Business Account (WABA)-level account info.

FieldRequiredDescription
businessAccountId✓ YesWABA ID.

get-verification-status

Returns Meta Business verification status.

FieldRequiredDescription
businessPortfolioId✓ YesBusiness Portfolio (Meta Business Manager) ID.
Contact vs. business profile: get-contact / update-contact-name act on someone you're messaging (contactPhoneNumber). get-business-profile / update-business-profile act on your own sending number (phoneNumberId).
